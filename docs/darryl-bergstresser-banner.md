# Darryl Bergstresser banner

The original 900 × 300 PNG is stored at `ad-assets/darryl-bergstresser-banner.png`. It appears in banner carousels for these seven county editions, including their county subpages:

- Oklahoma: Tulsa, Rogers, and Oklahoma counties.
- Texas: Potter, Randall, Midland, and Ector counties.

Clicking the banner opens `https://www.barrettfinancial.com/`. The banner is not registered as a square or in-feed ad. Existing partner-directory behavior also lists this advertiser globally and under its seven counties.

The shared banner carousel now includes every eligible banner instead of truncating the list to four. This allows the new creative and existing banners to remain reachable in counties with more than four advertisers; existing ordering and rotation timing are preserved.
